Who We Are

Transforming Health Care Together

{{ site.organisation_name }} grew out of a simple conviction: the distance between a health product and the person who needs it should never decide whether they live. What began as a bridge between communities and the health system has become a model for last-mile care.

We work with the three authorities that shape health at the last mile — community, government and the health system itself — so that the gains we make together outlast any single programme.
